# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite + React + TypeScript, deployed on Vercel (confirmed by the team).

## Users

- Owners, directors and administrative coordinators of small and medium businesses, public agencies and schools in Brazil who run operations on spreadsheets and WhatsApp and have no in-house IT team. They decide the purchase; they are not technical.
- Secondary: early-stage startups needing an MVP, and public or research institutions (grants, hackathons, university partnerships).

## Product Purpose

Landing page and portfolio for SUNA, a software house of 7 formalized members covering backend, frontend, data and infrastructure. The page presents what SUNA builds (custom web systems, platforms, data and reporting, infrastructure), shows delivered work, and turns visits into a WhatsApp conversation.

## Positioning

A complete team (code to infrastructure, plus support after delivery) at a price a small business can afford: the alternative to the freelancer who disappears and the consultancy that is too expensive.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Language: Brazilian Portuguese, close and direct tone, no technical jargon.
- Portfolio entries are placeholders for now; the team will fill in real projects later.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: SUNA ("suna" means sand in Japanese; the team frames the brand around the dune: shaped by the wind, but it stays).
- Logos supplied by the team: wordmark, full logo with "Software e Desenvolvimento", and the symbol (in `src/assets/brand/`).
- Official palette: Toffee #B78B5A, Manteiga #E5CFA5, Linho #F9EED9, Espresso #442D1D, Cereja #A23931.
- The team asked for a page that looks less AI-generated and works well on phones.

## Evidence on Hand

- Logos as PNG. No client logos, testimonials, metrics or case studies yet: do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

1. Speak to the decision-maker's problem before naming any technology.
2. Every claim on the page must be true for a 7-person team today.
3. The fastest path to a conversation (WhatsApp) is always one tap away.
4. Show real work as soon as it exists; mark placeholders clearly until then.
