# Surface: landing page + portfolio (src/App.tsx)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: owners and coordinators of SMBs, public agencies and schools in Maranhão and beyond, non-technical. Action: start a WhatsApp conversation. Proof on hand: none yet beyond the team itself; portfolio entries are labeled placeholders.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The SUNA symbol is a stone with raked sand lines. The page is that karesansui garden: lines of sand that flow around whatever is placed in them, as SUNA's software flows around the client's business. It refuses the software-house template (split hero with screenshot, three equal service cards, logo strip).

OWN-WORLD: Manteiga sand ground raked with Toffee lines; Espresso and Cereja as solid stones (rounded, irregular ellipses from the symbol); Espresso "night garden" bands for portfolio and footer; Cereja drenched field for contact. Type: Unbounded lowercase, like the logo wordmark, for display; Onest for text. No serif, no italic, no glass, no gradients.

STORY: The visitor recognises their own mess (spreadsheets, WhatsApp, vanished freelancer), sees what SUNA builds and how, sees a team of seven as seven stones, and opens WhatsApp with a message already written.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed canvas of raked sand. Headline "software que se molda ao seu negócio." large, lowercase, left, across about the left half (amended after review: Unbounded is very wide, display size is capped at 6rem, and the symbol stone needs the right third). The symbol as the garden's main stone at right, lines bending around it and around the pointer. Primary action "Conversar no WhatsApp" under the subline, Cereja. Nav: wordmark, four links, WhatsApp action.

FORM: Jardim de areia (karesansui), position 1 on my ordered list, chosen by the user as IMPECCABLE'S PICK over assigned candidate 6 (olaria). seed:3219af2f9af910fb. Degraded roll, no challengers. Signature interaction: pointer acts as a stone the raked lines part around and settle back from. Motion grammar: lines settle with spring easing; sections reveal from a visible rest state only.
